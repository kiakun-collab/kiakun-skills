# Pitch Visual Patterns

Use these as composition families, not fixed templates. Choose the pattern that best explains the current claim.

## Contents

- One Hero + Evidence
- Claim + Reasons
- Equal Visual Actions
- Dual Concept Comparison
- Product System
- UI Trigger + Outcome
- Social Content Wall
- Object-in-Use Story
- Full-Slide Guardrails
- Cross-Category Adaptation

## One Hero + Evidence

Use when one experience, product, place, or activation carries the central idea.

- one large hero image
- two to four supporting details or proof points
- separate copy area with clear hierarchy
- best for launches, environments, retail, events, travel, hospitality, and product stories

## Claim + Reasons

Use when a location, partnership, audience, or channel choice needs justification.

- large contextual image or triptych
- two to four rationale blocks
- every rationale tied to a distinct visible detail
- best for location strategy, audience fit, partnership logic, and market entry

## Equal Visual Actions

Use when every execution step matters.

- equal columns or rows
- one image per action
- short action heading and outcome
- no single decorative image pretending to represent all steps
- best for activation mechanics, customer journeys, rollout plans, and content matrices

## Dual Concept Comparison

Use when two creative routes share one mechanism.

- common proposition and constraints at the top
- two equal visual routes with distinct scenes
- a restrained shared outcome or decision line
- best for route selection, creative territories, design alternatives, and scenario comparison

## Product System

Use when multiple components form an offer.

- dominant product family or system image
- detail views for hero items
- usage or exchange scene to show value beyond a catalog
- best for packaging, merchandise, services, membership benefits, and retail systems

## UI Trigger + Outcome

Use when the idea depends on a digital interaction.

- one or two large device or interface states
- explicit user input and system response
- optional real-world or commercial outcome as secondary evidence
- best for search, chat, check-in, recommendation, loyalty, booking, and campaign mechanics

## Social Content Wall

Use when distribution formats are the idea.

- platform-credible content samples
- differentiated creator roles or content angles
- cover text, title, author, and engagement areas in correct hierarchy
- exclude metrics or controls that are not part of the brief
- best for creator strategies, UGC, seeding, earned media, and launch amplification

## Object-in-Use Story

Use when a prop, package, wearable, or collectible enables the behavior.

- structural product view
- hand-held or in-context use
- detail view of the functional feature
- best for photo props, packaging, collectibles, retail tools, and interactive merchandise

## Full-Slide Guardrails

For complete slide images:

- reserve a clean title zone
- establish one primary message
- keep visual evidence larger than decorative copy
- use no more than three hierarchy levels
- prevent images from covering essential text
- avoid layouts that cannot be recreated in a deck when editability is an implied goal
- do not add page numbers, badges, captions, or labels that the brief did not request

## Cross-Category Adaptation

Change the evidence, not the workflow:

- automotive: vehicle, road behavior, cabin, retail, charging, ownership moment
- beauty/fashion: product texture, application, model context, packaging, retail display
- food/beverage: product appetite, serving ritual, venue, packaging, social moment
- technology/SaaS: interface state, workflow, human outcome, device environment
- finance/professional services: credible situation, data evidence, service interaction, trust signals
- property/hospitality/travel: place identity, arrival, circulation, amenities, guest behavior
- entertainment/IP: world-building cues, fan behavior, collectible or event mechanics, authorization boundaries

Do not import visual codes from one category into another without evidence from the current brief.
